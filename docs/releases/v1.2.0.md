The native port of Sega's 1993 arcade game *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*: not an emulator, but the game's own
program decompiled into C ([segasonic-decomp](https://github.com/cheviman/segasonic-decomp)) and built for
Windows, Linux and macOS, and now, made on your computer, for the Nintendo Switch and Android.

**No game data is included.** You need your own MAME ROM set: `sonic.zip`, *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*
(Japan, rev. C). Put it beside the game, or drop it on the window the game opens the first time: the game
checks it and takes out what it uses, once.

| System | Package |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, x64 | `segasonic-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux, x64 | `segasonic-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel | `segasonic-macos-universal.zip` |
| Nintendo Switch | made by `SegaSonic NSP`, in each package above |
| Android 7 or later | made by `SegaSonic APK`, in each package above |

What is new since v1.1.0:

- **Nintendo Switch.** `SegaSonic NSP` makes an `.nsp` of the game's Switch build (the folder `switch`
  beside it), your `sonic.zip` and your console's keys (`prod.keys`), to install as any homebrew `.nsp` on a
  console with Atmosphère and signature patches. 16:9, the controls fixed, HOME leaves it. Played on Eden;
  not yet on a console: reports are welcome.
- **Android.** `SegaSonic APK` makes an APK of the game's Android build (the folder `android` beside it) and
  your `sonic.zip`, signed with a key of yours that it makes the first time and keeps in your own folder:
  keep it, so each new APK installs over the last one. 16:9, touch controls (a stick where your thumb
  lands, A and START) or a Bluetooth or USB game controller; the pause has CONTINUE and EXIT.
- Both tools have a window: drop your files on it (the keys, `sonic.zip`, a picture for your own icon),
  choose where the file goes, and make it. A picture of yours named `icon.jpg` beside them is the icon of
  both. From a command line they work as before.
- **Anbernic's H700 handhelds** (the RG40XX H, stock firmware): a folder for `Roms/PORTS`, built from the
  source with Zig (see the README). 4:3, 60 frames a second, MENU leaves it, the volume keys work.
- The board's video is drawn on several cores on every system, a band of rows each: the frames are the same,
  as the 98 scripted runs show.

What the Switch's and Android's tools make carries your ROM's assets: it is yours alone, never share it.

SDL2 and the C runtime are built into the executables: nothing else to install. The macOS apps are not
signed by a developer: the first time, open one with right click → Open (on macOS 15 and later, try to
open it, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).

The game was checked frame by frame against MAME, picture and sound, on Windows and Linux; the macOS build
has not been played on a Mac yet: reports are welcome.
